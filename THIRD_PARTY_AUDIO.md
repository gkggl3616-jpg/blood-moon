The untouched recording is archived in `app/src/main/assets/media/reload-original.wav`.
The APK uses `reload.ogg`, trimmed to the existing 1.4 second reload window, mono,
44.1 kHz and loudness-normalized without clipping. The recording was made with an
airsoft gun according to the author; it is recorded foley rather than a synthesized UI sound.
